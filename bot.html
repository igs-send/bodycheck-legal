What it does

Business cards

A simple one-page presence for a shop or a maker, built in a few minutes inside the bot.

Open directory

A searchable map of local places. Anyone can add somewhere they like and share it with others.

Community

Entrepreneurs find each other, recommend each other, and reach customers who are already nearby.

Why we started it

The smallest businesses have the hardest time getting online, and most of the tools built for them are really built for someone much bigger. A café with four tables does not need a content management system. It needs to be findable.

So the first step was a Telegram bot: a shared directory of local places that anyone can add to. It grew out of Portugal's tech community and was incubated at VilaWork.
