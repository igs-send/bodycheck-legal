About

AquiLink started in 2023 with a small Telegram bot that put local Portuguese businesses on the map, incubated at VilaWork. The idea behind it was that useful software does not have to be big, and that the people it serves should not have to hand over much to use it.

Body Check is where that thinking went next, and it is what the studio works on today. Health apps tend to ask for an account, a subscription and your data before they give you anything back. We wanted the opposite — something that works the moment you open it, keeps your records on your own device, and asks for nothing.

We are a small team, and we like it that way — it keeps the work close to the people who use it. AquiLink is based and registered in Figueira da Foz, Portugal.
